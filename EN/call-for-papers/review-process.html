Review Process

How Papers Are Reviewed

ACL 2019 uses double-blind peer review. Reviewers evaluate the technical contribution, originality, clarity, soundness and relevance of each submission while following the conference’s anonymity rules.

Presentation Type

Accepted papers may be assigned to oral or poster presentation formats. Presentation format reflects the nature of the work and program constraints rather than a separate quality tier in the proceedings.

Reviewer Guidance

Reviewers should provide clear, constructive feedback and justify their recommendation. Additional details are available on the Instructions for Reviewers page.

Author Checklist

  • Remove identifying author information.
  • Keep claims supported by evidence and evaluation.
  • Follow page limits and formatting requirements.
  • Disclose relevant multiple submissions as required by conference policy.
