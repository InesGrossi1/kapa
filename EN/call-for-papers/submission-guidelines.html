Submission Guidelines

Preparing a Submission

ACL 2019 welcomes substantial, original and unpublished work in computational linguistics and natural language processing. Authors should choose the paper format that best matches the contribution.

Long Papers

Long papers may contain up to eight pages of content, with references outside the content limit. Accepted long papers receive an additional content page for the final version.

Short Papers

Short papers may contain up to four pages of content, with references outside the content limit. Short papers are intended for focused contributions, work in progress, negative results, opinion pieces and compact applications.

Anonymity

Submissions are reviewed double-blind. Author names and affiliations must be omitted, and self-references should be written so they do not reveal the authors’ identity.

Supplementary Material

Software, data and supplementary material may accompany a paper, but the paper should remain understandable and reviewable without requiring reviewers to inspect those materials.

See the complete Main Conference Call for Papers for the official wording and policies.
